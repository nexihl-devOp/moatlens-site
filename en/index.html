Stance

What MoatLens deliberately does not do

The most important part of an analysis app is sometimes what it leaves out.

Not in the app

  • No buy or sell recommendations
  • No price targets, no trading signals
  • No red panic notifications
  • No promises of returns
  • No brokerage connection needed

Instead

  • A quality score computed by fixed rules, identical for every user
  • Every factor expandable and explained rather than asserted
  • An AI that only describes — it deliberately never sees the share price
  • No verdict at all rather than a shaky one when the data is too thin
  • A calm, dark design without “buy now” buttons

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is fundamental analysis?

Fundamental analysis looks at the company behind the share instead of the price chart: revenue, earnings, margins, free cash flow, debt and return on capital over several years. The aim is to understand how the business works and how stable it is. MoatLens shows these figures across ten years and explains each one in everyday language.

Does MoatLens give buy recommendations?

No. MoatLens gives no buy or sell recommendations, no price targets and no trading signals. The app describes metrics and business models and explains what they mean — the conclusion stays with you. MoatLens is not investment advice, it is an analysis and education tool.

What is the MoatLens Score?

The score combines eight quality factors of a company into one picture, from “Exceptional” to “Weak”. It is computed from fundamentals by fixed rules, is identical for every user and can be opened factor by factor. It describes the strength of the business — not whether a share should be bought.

What does MoatLens cost?

Starting is free. Premium unlocks the AI-assisted analyses and the annual-report summaries — either as a subscription or via one-off credit packs. No brokerage connection is required.

Which devices and countries?

MoatLens is currently available for Android on Google Play, in the EU and the UK. Languages: German, English, French and Spanish. More countries and an iOS version are planned.

Where does the data come from?

The fundamental data is licensed from Financial Modeling Prep. The quality score is computed deterministically from it by fixed rules. The AI explains the numbers but deliberately never sees the share price — so it cannot make any statement about buying, selling or timing.

Who is behind MoatLens?

MoatLens is a one-person project from Germany. The app is young and gets better with every user — feedback goes straight to the person building it: support@moatlens.app.
