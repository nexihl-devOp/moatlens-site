Business model

This rating looks at the resilience of the business model – how predictable, simple and crisis-proof the business is.

Why does this matter?

A predictable business with recurring revenue is easier to assess and weathers hard times more easily. Models that depend heavily on a single product, customer or on the economic cycle are more fragile – even when current figures look good.

What MoatLens looks at:

Is revenue recurring and stable? How great is the dependence on single factors? Do you understand how the company makes money? A simple, predictable model marks it up, a fragile or opaque one marks it down.
